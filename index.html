SEC_03 // INTEGRATION

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with third-party service providers necessary to operate the App:

Data Storage & Retention

Your uploaded photos and roasts are stored securely in our database.

  • History Preference: You can toggle "Save Roast History" off. If toggled off, photos and roasts are not saved to your profile history.
